## 🌟 About iGrad AI Labs

<div class="card">

<p>

<b>iGrad AI Labs</b> is a technology-driven learning platform that provides industry-oriented internships focused on Open Source Development, Artificial Intelligence, Workflow Automation, Cloud Computing and Technical Documentation.

The internship program helps students bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practices by working on practical projects, modern development tools and collaborative workflows.

</p>

</div>
