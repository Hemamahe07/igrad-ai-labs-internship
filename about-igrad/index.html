    🏢 About iGrad AI Labs

    Empowering Students Through Learning, Innovation & Open Source

    🌟 About iGrad AI Labs¶

    iGrad AI Labs is a technology-driven learning platform that provides industry-oriented internships focused on Open Source Development, Artificial Intelligence, Workflow Automation, Cloud Computing and Technical Documentation. The internship program helps students bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practices by working on practical projects, modern development tools and collaborative workflows.

    🎯 Vision & Mission¶

    🎯

    Vision

    To empower students with practical technical skills and prepare them for successful careers in technology.
    🚀

    Mission

    To provide hands-on learning through internships, innovation, collaboration and real-world project experience.
